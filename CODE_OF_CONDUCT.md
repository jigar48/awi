# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and inclusive. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexual harassment and deliberate disruption are unacceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may remove harmful contributions, warn, restrict or ban participants according to severity and recurrence. Protect affected participants and preserve confidentiality. Report conduct issues privately through a maintainer channel listed on the repository owner's profile; do not publish private details in issues. Maintainers should recuse themselves from reports about their own conduct.
